Approach

Methodology

1
Data Acquisition

Copernicus CAMS pollutant rasters and CORINE land cover change vectors downloaded for Hungary, 2021 & 2023.

2
Processing (Python / GDAL)

Change detection, zonal statistics extraction per LCC zone (Stable / Gain / Loss), and raster tile generation for web display.

3
Spatial Analysis

Transition matrices computed for each cover type, linking gain/loss source–destination pairs with pollutant zone statistics.

4
Visualisation

Results published as an interactive WebGIS (Mapbox GL JS + GeoTIFF.js) and statistical charts (Chart.js) on this site.
